# Urban Explorer Research: Spokane

A Comprehensive Walking Guide to Spokane's Urban Core

Welcome to Spokane, a city defined by its powerful river, historic brick architecture, and fiercely independent spirit. While it may not have the scale of a major metropolis, its charm lies in its distinct, walkable neighborhoods, each offering a unique slice of Inland Northwest life. This guide is for the urban explorer who prefers to discover a city on foot, seeking out the local haunts, hidden histories, and quiet moments that exist just beyond the tourist trail.

We'll explore three unique districts, all within a comfortable walking radius of the city center, focusing on the independent spots that give Spokane its character.

### **1. Browne's Addition: A Stroll Through History**

**The Vibe:** Just west of downtown, Browne's Addition is Spokane's oldest and most storied neighborhood. Walking its leafy streets feels like stepping into the city's Gilded Age past, with grand Victorian and Queen Anne mansions lining the avenues. It's a quiet, residential area with a palpable sense of history, anchored by a few beloved, long-standing local institutions.

**Coffee & Drinks**

*   **The Elk Public House:** A quintessential neighborhood pub housed in a former pharmacy building. It’s a community cornerstone, perfect for a local pint and conversation.
    *   **What makes it special:** The atmosphere is pure, unpretentious neighborhood tavern. It's the kind of place where everyone seems to know each other.
    *   **Photo Challenge:** Capture a photo that highlights the building's past life as a pharmacy—look for architectural remnants or interior details.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Find the oldest-looking piece of decor or signage inside the pub.

**Food & Restaurants**

*   **The Elk Public House:** While a great drink spot, The Elk is locally famous for its food. The menu is packed with elevated pub fare, but one item stands above the rest.
    *   **What makes it special:** Locals will tell you it serves one of the best Reubens you'll ever have, with corned beef that is slow-cooked on-site and shredded to perfection. Don't skip the spicy corn pasta salad on the side.
    *   **Photo Challenge:** The "perfect bite" shot: a cross-section of the legendary Reuben sandwich.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Ask your server what year the pub opened in this location.
*   **Italia Trattoria:** An unassuming but exceptional Italian restaurant that showcases the chef's skill. It’s a spot for a more deliberate, memorable meal.
    *   **What makes it special:** Helmed by a James Beard Award-nominated chef, the menu features regionally inspired Italian food, including exquisite fresh pasta and gnocchi.
    *   **Photo Challenge:** Focus on the plating. Capture the artistry of a pasta dish against the restaurant's simple, elegant backdrop.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Identify an ingredient on the menu that is sourced from the Pacific Northwest.

**Urban Discoveries**

*   **The "Hot Carly" Ghost Sign:** On the eastern edge of the neighborhood, where it meets downtown, look up. The old Hotel Carlyle building features a faded sign where parts of the name have worn away, leaving the iconic and locally beloved "Hot Carly."
    *   **What makes it special:** This accidental landmark is a celebrated piece of Spokane's urban lore. A recent attempt to paint over it was met with public outcry, cementing its status as a cherished quirk.
    *   **Photo Challenge:** Frame a shot that contrasts the weathered, historic "Hot Carly" sign with a modern element of the city skyline.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Nearby, you'll see other faded "ghost signs" on brick buildings. Try to decipher the words on one of the other historic advertisements.
*   **Victorian Architecture Tour:** The real treasure of Browne's Addition is the architecture itself. Spend an hour simply wandering the residential streets off W Pacific Ave and W 2nd Ave.
    *   **What makes it special:** You'll find a stunning concentration of well-preserved mansions from the late 19th century, showcasing intricate woodwork, turrets, and wraparound porches.
    *   **Photo Challenge:** Create a photo series of three unique and ornate front porch designs you discover.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Find a home with a historical plaque and learn the name of the original owner.

### **2. Downtown & The Davenport Arts District: Art Deco and Hidden Alleys**

**The Vibe:** This is the energetic heart of Spokane, where the city's history and future collide. The Davenport Arts District is the cultural core, defined by magnificent, restored theaters and a cluster of galleries. The vibe is creative and vibrant, with the best discoveries—from hidden bars to world-class tacos—tucked away in plain sight.

**Coffee & Drinks**

*   **Hogwash Whiskey Den:** A cozy, subterranean bar that feels like a well-kept secret. It's a place for connoisseurs and those looking for a thoughtfully crafted drink.
    *   **What makes it special:** Praised by locals as a spot that "always hits," it offers a curated and impressive selection of whiskey and inventive cocktails in an intimate setting.
    *   **Photo Challenge:** Use the low light to your advantage. Capture the warm glow of the bar and the silhouette of the unique glassware.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Find the most unusual or rare whiskey on their menu.
*   **Vieux Carre:** An atmospheric spot with a New Orleans-inspired theme, celebrated for both its food and its cocktails.
    *   **What makes it special:** The name (French for "French Quarter") sets the mood. Locals love the unique atmosphere, which transports you far from the Inland Northwest.
    *   **Photo Challenge:** Capture a detail—a piece of decor, a menu font, a cocktail garnish—that perfectly encapsulates the New Orleans vibe.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Ask the bartender to recommend a classic New Orleans cocktail that they make especially well.

**Food & Restaurants**

*   **Cochinito Taqueria:** This is not your standard taco shop. It's a chef-driven taqueria where fine-dining techniques meet the humble tortilla.
    *   **What makes it special:** Founded by a high-end chef, the tacos are artfully constructed with unique ingredients like octopus, and the homemade tortillas are a standout. It's a consistent local favorite.
    *   **Photo Challenge:** An overhead "flat lay" of your taco selection, showcasing the vibrant colors and ingredients.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Find the most unexpected ingredient on the taco menu.
*   **Durkins:** A refined dining spot that delivers consistently excellent, flavor-forward American cuisine.
    *   **What makes it special:** It's a go-to for locals who want a guaranteed great meal without fuss. It's known for its quality and execution across the entire menu.
    *   **Photo Challenge:** Capture the contrast between the elegant dining room upstairs and the more casual, moody bar downstairs.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Durkins is known for its burgers. Find out what kind of cheese is used on their signature cheeseburger.

**Urban Discoveries**

*   **Martin Woldson Theater at the Fox:** An absolute architectural masterpiece. Even if you don't see a show, the building itself is a landmark worth admiring from the outside.
    *   **What makes it special:** It was meticulously restored to its original 1931 Art Deco glory. The sweeping lines and intricate details are a stunning example of the era's design.
    *   **Photo Challenge:** At twilight ("blue hour"), capture the theater's glowing marquee and the intricate patterns of its facade.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Locate the "Spokane Symphony" lettering on the building's exterior.
*   **Bing Crosby Theater:** Named for Spokane's most famous crooner, this historic theater adds to the district's cultural weight.
    *   **What makes it special:** Originally the Metropolitan Performing Arts Center, its 1988 restoration and subsequent renaming honor a deep piece of local history.
    *   **Photo Challenge:** Frame a shot that includes both the theater's vertical sign and a piece of the surrounding historic streetscape.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Find the year the theater was renamed to honor Bing Crosby.
*   **First Friday Artwalk Hub:** While the official event is monthly, the area around W 1st Ave and S Monroe St is the permanent epicenter of Spokane's gallery scene.
    *   **What makes it special:** On any day of the week, you can wander this area and peer into the windows of numerous local art galleries, getting a sense of the regional creative pulse.
    *   **Photo Challenge:** Capture a reflection of a historic building in the window of a modern art gallery.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Count how many art galleries you can spot within a two-block radius of the Fox Theater.

### **3. The South Perry District: Community Charm and Local Flavor**

**The Vibe:** South of the river and downtown, the South Perry District is a perfect example of a revitalized neighborhood commercial district. It’s a single, vibrant street flanked by quiet residential avenues of charming Craftsman homes. The vibe is laid-back, family-friendly, and fiercely local. This is where Spokanites go on a Saturday morning for coffee and pastries or for a weeknight pizza and beer.

**Coffee & Drinks**

*   **Rockwood Bakery:** A beloved bakery and cafe situated in a former neighborhood grocery store, a short, pleasant walk from the main Perry Street drag.
    *   **What makes it special:** It's a full scratch bakery known for its incredible quiche (with daily meat and vegetarian options) and an addictive chicken chipotle sandwich. The cozy, sophisticated atmosphere makes it a prime meeting spot.
    *   **Photo Challenge:** Capture the texture and layers of a slice of their famous quiche.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Ask what the building was before it became a bakery.
*   **The Grain Shed:** A brewery and bakery that embodies the district's focus on craft and quality. It’s a casual hangout with a serious approach to its products.
    *   **What makes it special:** Locals rave about its "hole in the wall vibe with flavor." It's particularly known for its Monday pizza nights, which have become a neighborhood ritual.
    *   **Photo Challenge:** If it's Monday, get a shot of the pizza. Otherwise, focus on the tap handles and the rustic, industrial interior.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Find out what types of local grains they use in their beer or bread.

**Food & Restaurants**

*   **Kismet:** A small, unassuming spot that packs a flavorful punch, embodying the "hidden gem" spirit.
    *   **What makes it special:** It's a true local's spot, recommended specifically because its neighborhood setting is part of its charm. It’s the definition of a place you’d only find if someone in the know told you about it.
    *   **Photo Challenge:** Take a photo of the restaurant's exterior that shows how it blends into the surrounding residential neighborhood.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** See if the menu is written on a chalkboard or a printed page.

**Urban Discoveries**

*   **Manito Park:** A short walk from the South Perry business district, this is one of Spokane's crown jewels. While large and popular, it contains quieter, more secluded areas perfect for discovery.
    *   **What makes it special:** The park is a massive, beautifully maintained space. Instead of heading for the main attractions, seek out a quiet bench with a view over the gardens for a moment of peace.
    *   **Photo Challenge:** Find a secluded path or "secret garden" area and capture a photo with no other people in it.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Find three different types of trees in the park and identify them.
*   **Craftsman Home Architecture:** The streets branching off Perry Street (like E 10th Ave and E 11th Ave) are a living museum of early 20th-century residential architecture.
    *   **What makes it special:** This is where you feel the true neighborhood character. The area is filled with beautifully maintained Craftsman bungalows, each with unique details like gabled roofs, inviting front porches, and handcrafted woodwork.
    *   **Photo Challenge:** Create a collage of three different front doors, each with a unique color or design.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Find a house with a "Little Free Library" in its front yard.
*   **Perry Street's Independent Shops:** The main discovery is the street itself. Walk the few blocks of S Perry Street that make up the business district and take note of the businesses.
    *   **What makes it special:** The lack of national chains is striking. It's a collection of small, independent businesses—a bike shop, a wellness studio, a vintage store—that give the area its strong community identity.
    *   **Photo Challenge:** Take a wide shot of the street that captures the "main street" feel and the variety of storefronts.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Identify three businesses on the street that are not a cafe, bar, or restaurant.
