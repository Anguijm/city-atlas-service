### **District 1: Downtown & Smoky Hill River Corridor**

This area is the historic and cultural heart of Salina. A walk here combines encounters with local history, community arts, and the surprisingly dramatic landscape carved by the Smoky Hill River. The vibe is a blend of small-town civic pride and natural beauty, perfect for an afternoon of thoughtful exploration.

**Interesting Spots & Hidden Gems**

1.  **Smoky Hill Museum**
    *   **What Makes It Worth Visiting:** This isn't just a dusty collection of artifacts; it's the city's attic, preserving the story of Salina's development, agricultural roots, and people since 1879. It offers a tangible connection to the region's history through its exhibits and educational programs.
    *   **Location:** 211 W Iron Ave, Salina, KS
    *   **Photo Challenge:** Find the most unusual or surprising artifact on display and capture a photo that tells its story in a single frame.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Locate an exhibit related to the Great Flood of 1951, a pivotal event in the city's history.

2.  **Salina Community Theatre (SCT)**
    *   **What Makes It Worth Visiting:** A cornerstone of the local arts scene, this regionally-acclaimed theater is a testament to Salina's vibrant community spirit. While seeing a show is the main event, the building itself is a hub of creative energy.
    *   **Location:** 303 E Iron Ave, Salina, KS
    *   **Photo Challenge:** Capture the theater's marquee at dusk, playing with the contrast between the glowing lights and the evening sky.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Find the playbill or poster for the current season. Which show sounds the most intriguing or unexpected for a town-tier city?

3.  **Indian Rock Park**
    *   **What Makes It Worth Visiting:** This is Salina's best-kept secret for panoramic views. The park's main feature is a prominent hill, the highest point in the area, with dramatic 80-foot shale bluffs created during a 1950s flood control project. It offers a unique intersection of natural geology and human engineering.
    *   **Location:** Near the intersection of E. Magnolia Rd and S. Ohio St.
    *   **Photo Challenge:** Time your visit for the "golden hour" just before sunset. From the top of the hill, capture a sweeping panorama of Salina as the city lights begin to turn on.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Find the hiking trail that leads down to the river's edge and locate the fishing pier.

4.  **Tony's Pizza Events Center**
    *   **What Makes It Worth Visiting:** As the primary venue for large-scale events, this arena is a modern landmark that hosts everything from concerts to CIF indoor football games (Go, Salina Liberty!). Its scale is impressive for a city of Salina's size.
    *   **Location:** 800 The Midway, Salina, KS
    *   **Photo Challenge:** Frame a shot of the building's exterior that emphasizes its geometric lines and modern architecture against the vast Kansas sky.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Find a poster or digital sign for an upcoming event. Is it a sports tournament, a concert, or a trade show?

### **District 2: Kenwood Park Recreation Hub**

Located just south of the downtown core, this area is Salina's playground. The vibe is active, family-friendly, and centered around outdoor fun. It's a place where the community comes to cool off, play, and enjoy the open space.

**Interesting Spots & Hidden Gems**

1.  **Kenwood Cove Aquatic Park**
    *   **What Makes It Worth Visiting:** This is far more than a simple city pool. Designed as a regional attraction, this Caribbean-themed water park features over 1,400 feet of slides, a wave pool, and elaborate theming. It’s an ambitious and fun-filled project that showcases the city's commitment to quality-of-life amenities.
    *   **Location:** 701 Kenwood Park Dr, Salina, KS
    *   **Photo Challenge:** Use a slow shutter speed (if your camera/phone allows) to capture the motion of the water on a slide, creating a silky, blurred effect against the static structure.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Find the tallest slide tower. How many distinct slides start from its main platform?

2.  **Historic Kenwood Field Site**
    *   **What Makes It Worth Visiting:** While the original ballpark where Salina's minor league teams played from 1938-1952 is gone, the area is steeped in local sports history. Walking the grounds of Kenwood Park, you can imagine the era when the Salina Blue Jays, affiliated with the Philadelphia Phillies, played here. It’s a quiet nod to the city’s baseball past.
    *   **Location:** Within Kenwood Park, near the aquatic center.
    *   **Photo Challenge:** Find a wide-open green space within the park and take a low-angle shot that evokes the feeling of an old-time baseball outfield.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Look for any plaques or markers in the park that might commemorate its long history of recreation and sport.

3.  **Film History Landmark: *Picnic* Filming Location**
    *   **What Makes It Worth Visiting:** For classic film buffs, Salina holds a special place in cinematic history. Scenes for the 1955 classic *Picnic*, starring William Holden and Kim Novak, were filmed here. While the specific mansion is a private residence, walking the historic neighborhoods around Kenwood Park allows you to soak in the atmosphere that Hollywood producers found so compelling.
    *   **Location:** General historic neighborhoods surrounding Kenwood Park.
    *   **Photo Challenge:** Capture a photo of a grand, historic home in the area that looks like it could have been the setting for the film's iconic party scene.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** The character Judy Barton in Hitchcock's *Vertigo* was also from Salina. Can you find a Maple Avenue on a local map?

### **District 3: West Salina Wildlife Gateway**

This area represents the transition from the city grid to the wide-open spaces of Kansas. It requires a longer walk or short drive (it is 9.7km, just within the 10km radius) but rewards visitors with a top-tier wildlife experience that feels a world away from downtown.

**Interesting Spots & Hidden Gems**

1.  **Rolling Hills Wildlife Adventure**
    *   **What Makes It Worth Visiting:** This is a unique combination of a public zoo and a wildlife museum, offering a surprisingly comprehensive experience. The facility is known for its spacious animal exhibits, impressive art gallery, and educational focus, making it a destination that punches well above its weight for a city of Salina's size.
    *   **Location:** 625 N Hedville Rd, Salina, KS (approx. 9.7 km / 6 miles west of downtown)
    *   **Photo Challenge:** Find an animal exhibit with a water feature and wait for the perfect moment to capture the animal's reflection in the water.
    *   **Scavenger Hunt:** Inside the wildlife museum, find the exhibit for the animal that is geographically farthest from Kansas.
